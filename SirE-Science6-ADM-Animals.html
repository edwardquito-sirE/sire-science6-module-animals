What is a Vertebrate?

A vertebrate is an animal that has a backbone (spinal column) made of bone or cartilage.

The backbone protects the spinal cord and helps the animal move.

🐕🦴
🐌

What is an Invertebrate?

An invertebrate is an animal that does NOT have a backbone.

Invertebrates come in many shapes and sizes. Most animals on Earth are invertebrates!

The 5 Kinds of Vertebrates

Mammals

🐃

Have hair or fur. Feed their babies milk.

Example: Carabao

Birds

🦅

Have feathers, wings and lay eggs.

Example: Eagle

Reptiles

🦎

Have dry, scaly skin.

Example: Bayawak

Amphibians

🐸

Live on land and in water.

Example: Palaka (Frog)

Fish

🐟

Live in water. Have fins and gills.

Example: Tilapia

Vertebrates vs. Invertebrates

Vertebrates Invertebrates
  • Have a backbone
  • Made of bone
  • Usually larger
  • Examples: dog, eagle, frog, tilapia
  • No backbone
  • No bones
  • Many small, some tiny
  • Examples: butterfly, snail, ant, crab
